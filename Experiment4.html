Information Technology Resource Centre (ITRC)
About The Department

Empowering Innovation Through State-of-the-Art Technology

Information Technology Resource Centre (ITRC) was established in 2005 under the World Bank programme (TEQIP-I) and represents a cornerstone of excellence among state engineering universities in Uttar Pradesh.

The objective of the centre is to provide unified, cutting-edge IT infrastructure under one roof for students, faculty, and research scholars. The local area network spanning the university campus is fully operated and monitored from this central hub, remaining open 24 hours daily with high-availability server racks and dedicated high-speed links.

Centralized campus-wide high-speed network management
High-performance computing labs for AI, Web, and Cloud research
Specialized smart seminar halls and multimedia e-learning resources
